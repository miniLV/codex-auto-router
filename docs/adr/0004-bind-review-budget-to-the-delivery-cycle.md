# Bind Delivery Budget to the Delivery Cycle

Status: Superseded by the current Runtime Router Policy.

The three-round Delivery Budget belongs to one user-visible goal's Delivery Cycle, not to a Worker, model lane, or Task Packet. When Luna ends and Root creates the one permitted fresh Terra fallback, the new child inherits the remaining budget; correcting the Task Packet also does not reset it. Automatic replacement Workers are not permitted. This prevents Root-mediated operational transitions from becoming implicit retry-budget escapes and does not imply direct Luna-to-Terra conversion.
